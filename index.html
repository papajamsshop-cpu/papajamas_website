About us

A record shop that
puts out records too.

PAPA JAMS started as a pile of crates and a tape machine that never stopped. Today the shop holds more than 800 second-hand references, a Discogs store that grows every day, and a label pressing small runs.

Everything is graded by hand, played before it goes on the rack, and packed the same day. If you are looking for a specific pressing, ask — chances are it is behind the counter.
